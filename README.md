# Dissonance

**Dissonance** is a rhythm-based RPG where you survive on a grid arena by reading attack patterns that move in sync with the music. This game is heavily inspired by [Crypt of the NecroDancer](https://store.steampowered.com/app/247080/Crypt_of_the_NecroDancer/) and [Asterika](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3690850/Asterika_Phantom_Rose_Refrain/).

In the year of 3067, the world still moves like it should, but it no longer pulses. Music exists, but its soul has long been dead. One day, a high school girl named **Suis** stumbles upon an old recording in her school library that makes her discover *music*.

When she begins creating music of her own, something awakens from the silence: The Dissonant, entities born from melodies that died before anyone could hear them. Suis need to face them one by one, not with weapons, but with rhythm.

## Controls

- `WASD` - For moving in the shard's arena
- `Esc` - Pause
- `Space` - Continuing in VN Mode

## Dissonant Shards

There are 3 shards you need to find for The Dissonant's body.

1. **Lumen** - Holding the genre of 80's City Pop
2. **Aria** - Holding the genre of Classical Music
3. **Tetra** - Holding the genre of EDM
